Features

Everything your agency needs

Built with 100+ years of combined EMS, fire, and IT experience.

📷

Camera barcode scanning New

Tap "Scan" and point your phone at any bag's barcode — it captures instantly. Works with Code 128, Code 39, QR, UPC, and more. Manual entry fallback always available.

📋

Log bags in seconds

Enter drug name, barcode, unit, medic, and call number in one simple form. Date and time pre-fill automatically. Every entry is saved locally — no internet required.

🔍

Scan-to-search history New

In the History tab, scan any bag's barcode to instantly look up its full chain of custody — who logged it, which unit, which call. Results highlight the matching barcode.

📦

Live inventory tracking

Each bag you log automatically deducts from inventory. Get alerts when any drug drops below 50 bags. One-tap reorder via email to Blaster.

📄

One-click PDF export

Generate a professional, audit-ready PDF report with your full log, agency contact info, and page numbers — instantly. Landscape layout, DEA-ready formatting.

🏢

Multi-agency support

Manage multiple EMS agencies from a single app. Filter dashboards, reports, and history by agency. Each agency keeps its own inventory and contact info.

📊

Dynamic reports & charts

See which drugs are used most, which units use the most bags, top medics by activity, and daily trends. Filter by agency and any month — populated from your real data.

✈️

Offline-first & installable New

The app installs to your home screen and runs 100% offline. A Service Worker caches everything — log bags even in areas with no signal. Data backed up via JSON/CSV export.

💾

Your data, your control

Export everything as JSON or CSV anytime. Import on any device. The app reminds you to back up regularly. No lock-in, no subscriptions, no cloud required.

Camera Barcode Scanning — Built for the Field
